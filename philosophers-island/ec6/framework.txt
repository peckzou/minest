TEXES CORE SUBJECTS EC-6 (391) — A STUDY TEXT

A study text organized by the official exam framework of TExES Core Subjects EC-6 (391): five subject exams and forty-four competencies. The structure, numbers and titles follow the framework; every explanation is written for this study hall in plain language, with a Chinese summary and key terms for each competency. It is not the framework's own wording. The current exam may be taken until 31 August 2027.

考纲结构（五门分考、44 条能力项、编号与标题）依据官方考纲；每条的讲解、中文要点和关键词都是本馆自己写的学习文本，不是官方原文。

SUBJECT EXAM I — ENGLISH LANGUAGE ARTS AND READING (901)

英语语言艺术与阅读：45 题，1 小时 10 分，约占总分 21.5%。共 10 条能力项：001 口语；002 词的分析与识别；003 阅读流畅度；004 阅读理解与应用；005 词汇发展；006 阅读、探究与研究；007 书写规范；008 书面表达；009 观看与表现；010 读写发展的评估。

COMPETENCY 001 — ORAL LANGUAGE

要点：孩子的口语按语音、词义、语法和语用四条线发展；老师要据此设计说和听的活动，并照顾英语学习者。

Spoken language grows along four strands at once: sounds, word meanings, sentence structure and the social rules of talk. A strong teacher plans daily talk on purpose: think-pair-share, retelling, academic conversations with sentence stems, and listening tasks with a clear goal. For English learners, oral language is the bridge to reading, so new words are spoken, heard and used before they are read.

Key terms: phonology (语音), semantics (语义), syntax (句法), pragmatics (语用), active listening (主动倾听), English learners (英语学习者), sentence stems (句子支架).

COMPETENCY 002 — WORD ANALYSIS AND IDENTIFICATION SKILLS

要点：从字母与声音的对应到音节、词根词缀，学会把陌生的词拆开读出来；高频词要读得又快又准。

Decoding starts with the alphabetic principle, the idea that letters stand for sounds, and moves from single letter-sound pairs to blends, digraphs, vowel teams and the six syllable types. Older readers break long words into prefixes, roots and suffixes. High-frequency words, including irregular ones, should be recognized at sight. Phonics is taught explicitly and systematically, then practised in decodable text.

Key terms: alphabetic principle (字母拼读原则), phonics (自然拼读), syllable types (六种音节类型), digraphs (二合字母), morphology (构词法), high-frequency words (高频词), decodable text (可解码文本).

COMPETENCY 003 — READING FLUENCY

要点：流畅度包括准确、速度和语调；它是解码和理解之间的桥：读得省力，才有余力去懂。

Fluency is accuracy, rate and prosody together, and it matters because it frees attention for meaning. Teachers measure words correct per minute against grade-level norms and listen for phrasing and expression. Repeated reading, partner reading, readers theatre and modelled reading build fluency; wide independent reading keeps it growing.

Key terms: accuracy (准确), rate (速度), prosody (语调韵律), words correct per minute (每分钟正确词数), repeated reading (重复朗读), readers theatre (读者剧场).

COMPETENCY 004 — READING COMPREHENSION AND APPLICATIONS

要点：理解分字面、推断和评价三层；读前、读中、读后各有策略，还要会跨文本比较，并监控自己懂没懂。

Comprehension works at three levels: what the text says, what it implies, and what we think of it. Readers preview and predict before reading, monitor and clarify during reading, and summarize and respond after reading. Good readers compare ideas across texts and notice when meaning breaks down. Knowing the features of genres, from fables to informational text, guides how a text should be read.

Key terms: literal (字面理解), inferential (推断理解), evaluative (评价理解), metacognition (元认知), summarizing (概括), text structure (文本结构), genre (体裁).

COMPETENCY 005 — VOCABULARY DEVELOPMENT

要点：词汇要显性教，也要在上下文里学；分清日常词、学术词和学科专用词，并用构词和词源帮助记忆。

Word knowledge grows from wide reading and talk, but the most useful words also need direct teaching. Tier 2 academic words such as compare or analyze carry across subjects; Tier 3 words belong to one field. Students learn to use context clues, roots and affixes, word origins and reference tools, and to show depth of knowledge with word maps and semantic gradients.

Key terms: Tier 2 words (二级学术词), Tier 3 words (三级学科词), context clues (上下文线索), roots and affixes (词根词缀), word maps (词汇图), semantic gradient (语义梯度).

COMPETENCY 006 — READING, INQUIRY, AND RESEARCH

要点：会提出研究问题，找到并评估一手和二手资料，读懂图表地图，用证据得出结论。

Inquiry begins with a good question and a plan. Students locate sources, judge whether they are reliable, separate primary from secondary sources, take notes in their own words and read maps, charts and graphs as texts. They then synthesize what they found and present conclusions that are supported by evidence.

Key terms: research question (研究问题), primary source (一手资料), secondary source (二手资料), credibility (可信度), note-taking (做笔记), synthesis (综合).

COMPETENCY 007 — WRITING CONVENTIONS

要点：书写从涂画、字母串到规范拼写一步步发展；拼写、书写姿势、语法和标点要在真实写作中教。

Writing develops in stages, from scribbles and letter strings to invented spelling and conventional spelling. Spelling is taught as patterns, not lists alone. Young writers need correct pencil grip and letter formation. Grammar, capitalization and punctuation are best taught inside the student’s own writing, where they change meaning.

Key terms: invented spelling (自创拼写), spelling patterns (拼写规律), letter formation (字母书写), pencil grip (握笔), capitalization (大写), punctuation (标点).

COMPETENCY 008 — WRITTEN COMMUNICATION

要点：写作过程是构思、起草、修改、编辑、发表；要按读者和目的选择文体，并正确引用来源。

The writing process moves through prewriting, drafting, revising, editing and publishing, and real writers loop back. Revising is about ideas and organization; editing is about conventions. Students write narratives, explanations and arguments for different audiences and purposes, use technology to draft and share, and cite sources to avoid plagiarism.

Key terms: writing process (写作过程), revising (修改), editing (编辑), audience (读者对象), purpose (写作目的), plagiarism (抄袭).

COMPETENCY 009 — VIEWING AND REPRESENTING

要点：会解读和评价图像、影像与媒体信息，比较不同媒介，并自己做出图文或多媒体作品。

Images and media carry meaning just as words do. Students learn to interpret visual elements, ask who made a message and why, compare how print, film and digital media present the same story, and notice persuasive techniques. They also create their own visuals and multimedia products to communicate ideas.

Key terms: media literacy (媒介素养), visual elements (视觉元素), persuasive techniques (说服技巧), multimedia (多媒体), point of view (视角).

COMPETENCY 010 — ASSESSMENT OF DEVELOPING LITERACY

要点：用形成性和总结性评估判断阅读水平，分析读写错误，据此干预并与家庭沟通。

Assessment guides teaching. Running records and miscue analysis show which cues a reader uses; screeners and diagnostic tests find gaps; writing samples reveal spelling stages. Teachers match texts to independent, instructional and frustration levels, group students flexibly, plan targeted intervention, and share progress with families in plain language.

Key terms: formative assessment (形成性评估), summative assessment (总结性评估), running record (阅读记录), miscue analysis (误读分析), instructional level (教学水平), intervention (干预).

SUBJECT EXAM II — MATHEMATICS (902)

数学：40 题，1 小时 10 分，约占总分 19%。共 6 条能力项：001 数学教学；002 数的概念与运算；003 规律与代数；004 几何与测量；005 概率与统计；006 数学过程。

COMPETENCY 001 — MATHEMATICS INSTRUCTION

要点：从具体到图像再到抽象（CPA），用操作材料和技术，靠提问和数学讨论推动理解，并持续评估。

Children understand mathematics best when ideas move from concrete objects to pictures to symbols. Teachers choose manipulatives and technology on purpose, ask questions that make thinking visible, and run discussions where students explain and critique strategies. Ongoing assessment shows misconceptions early, and lessons connect mathematics to careers and daily life.

Key terms: concrete-representational-abstract (具体-图像-抽象), manipulatives (操作教具), math discourse (数学讨论), misconceptions (错误概念), formative assessment (形成性评估).

COMPETENCY 002 — NUMBER CONCEPTS AND OPERATIONS

要点：整数、分数、小数、百分数之间的转换与运算，位值与估算，数论里的因数、倍数与质数。

Place value is the backbone of the number system: each place is ten times the one to its right. Students compute with whole numbers, integers, fractions and decimals, move between fraction, decimal and percent forms, and estimate to check answers. Number theory topics include factors, multiples, primes and divisibility, and fractions are understood as numbers on a number line, not only as parts of a pizza.

Key terms: place value (位值), fractions (分数), decimals (小数), percent (百分数), estimation (估算), prime numbers (质数), number line (数轴).

COMPETENCY 003 — PATTERNS AND ALGEBRA

要点：用表格、图像、文字和式子表示关系与函数；会把问题写成方程，掌握线性关系与比例推理。

Algebra starts with noticing patterns and describing how one quantity depends on another. A relationship can be shown as a table, a graph, words or an equation, and students translate among them. Linear functions have a constant rate of change; proportional relationships pass through the origin. Writing expressions and equations from word problems is the heart of this competency.

Key terms: patterns (规律), function (函数), linear relationship (线性关系), rate of change (变化率), proportional reasoning (比例推理), equation (方程).

COMPETENCY 004 — GEOMETRY AND MEASUREMENT

要点：周长、面积、体积公式，图形性质、变换与对称，单位换算，钱币，坐标平面。

Geometry asks what shapes are like and how they move. Students classify figures by attributes, use formulas for perimeter, area and volume, and recognize congruence, similarity, translations, reflections, rotations and symmetry. Measurement means choosing units, converting within a system, and working with time, money and the coordinate plane.

Key terms: perimeter (周长), area (面积), volume (体积), transformations (图形变换), symmetry (对称), unit conversion (单位换算), coordinate plane (坐标平面).

COMPETENCY 005 — PROBABILITY AND STATISTICS

要点：收集、整理、展示数据；平均数、中位数、众数与极差；简单与复合概率、样本空间与组合。

Statistics turns questions into data. Students collect and organize data, choose displays such as bar graphs, line plots, stem-and-leaf plots and histograms, and describe data with mean, median, mode and range. Probability measures chance from 0 to 1; sample spaces and tree diagrams count outcomes, and compound events combine them.

Key terms: mean (平均数), median (中位数), mode (众数), range (极差), sample space (样本空间), compound probability (复合概率).

COMPETENCY 006 — MATHEMATICAL PROCESSES

要点：归纳与演绎推理、判断答案是否合理、多种表示之间的联系、数学交流，以及数学史与个人理财。

Doing mathematics means reasoning, not just calculating. Students make conjectures from examples and prove or test them, judge whether an answer is reasonable, connect representations, and explain thinking in words, pictures and symbols. The competency also includes the history of mathematics and personal financial literacy: earning, saving, budgeting, credit and interest.

Key terms: inductive reasoning (归纳推理), deductive reasoning (演绎推理), reasonableness (合理性), representations (多种表示), financial literacy (理财素养), problem solving (问题解决).

SUBJECT EXAM III — SOCIAL STUDIES (903)

社会研究：40 题，50 分，约占总分 19%。共 5 条能力项：001 社会研究教学；002 历史；003 地理与文化；004 经济；005 政府与公民。

COMPETENCY 001 — SOCIAL SCIENCE INSTRUCTION

要点：熟悉社会研究 TEKS 的年级衔接，用适龄方法、技术与资料教学，会制作地图图表，并做好评估。

Social studies in Texas follows the TEKS, which spiral from family and community in the early grades to Texas history in grade 4 and United States history in grade 5. Teachers use primary sources, maps, simulations and technology, integrate reading and writing, and assess with both formal tests and projects. Students learn why studying society prepares them to be citizens.

Key terms: TEKS (得州基础知识与技能标准), vertical alignment (纵向衔接), primary sources (一手史料), simulations (模拟活动), map skills (地图技能), citizenship (公民身份).

COMPETENCY 002 — HISTORY

要点：从前殖民时代的原住民到 21 世纪的得州史、美国史与世界史；时间顺序、史料评估与历史思维。

History moves from the first peoples of Texas and North America through exploration, colonization, the Texas Revolution, statehood, the Civil War and Reconstruction, industrialization, the world wars, the civil rights movement and recent times. Students order events in time, weigh sources, explain cause and effect, and see how individuals and groups shaped Texas and the nation.

Key terms: chronology (时间顺序), Texas Revolution (得州革命), Reconstruction (重建时期), civil rights movement (民权运动), cause and effect (因果关系), historical sources (历史资料).

COMPETENCY 003 — GEOGRAPHY AND CULTURE

要点：地理五大主题与地图技能，环境如何塑造定居与资源，人口迁移，以及文化、宗教、艺术与科技对社会的影响。

Geography studies where things are and why. The five themes are location, place, human-environment interaction, movement and region. Students read maps and globes, compare the regions of Texas, explain how landforms and climate shaped where people settled, and trace migration. Culture covers traditions, religion, language, the arts and how technology changes ways of life.

Key terms: five themes of geography (地理五大主题), latitude and longitude (经纬度), regions of Texas (得州地理区域), human-environment interaction (人地互动), migration (迁移), culture (文化).

COMPETENCY 004 — ECONOMICS

要点：稀缺与机会成本、经济制度与自由企业、供给与需求、经济指标，以及得州经济与全国和全球的联系。

Economics is about choices under scarcity: every choice has an opportunity cost. Students compare economic systems, explain how free enterprise, supply and demand and competition set prices, and read indicators such as GDP and unemployment. Texas’s economy grew from ranching and cotton to oil, technology and trade, and it is tied to national and global markets.

Key terms: scarcity (稀缺), opportunity cost (机会成本), free enterprise (自由企业), supply and demand (供给与需求), GDP (国内生产总值), trade (贸易).

COMPETENCY 005 — GOVERNMENT AND CITIZENSHIP

要点：民主的源头，地方、州和联邦政府的结构与财政，建国文件与权利法案，修宪、最高法院判例与公民责任。

American democracy draws on the Magna Carta, the Mayflower Compact and Enlightenment ideas. The Declaration of Independence states why, the Constitution states how: three branches, checks and balances, federalism and the Bill of Rights. Students compare national, Texas and local government, follow how laws and amendments are made, study landmark cases, and practise the rights and duties of citizens.

Key terms: Constitution (美国宪法), Bill of Rights (权利法案), checks and balances (分权制衡), federalism (联邦制), Texas Constitution (得州宪法), civic responsibility (公民责任).

SUBJECT EXAM IV — SCIENCE (904)

科学：45 题，55 分，约占总分 21.5%。共 18 条能力项：001 实验过程、器材与安全；002 科学史与科学本质；003 科学的影响；004 统一概念与过程；005 学习者与科学教学；006 科学评估；007 力与运动；008 物理性质与化学性质；009 能量与相互作用；010 能量转化与守恒；011 生物的结构与功能；012 繁殖与遗传机制；013 适应与演化；014 生物与环境；015 地球系统的结构与功能；016 地球系统的循环；017 天气与气候中的能量；018 太阳系与宇宙。

COMPETENCY 001 — LAB PROCESSES, EQUIPMENT AND SAFETY

要点：实验安全规则，化学品与标本的处理和处置，选择合适的工具，测量精度与误差，数据呈现与公制换算。

Safe science comes first: goggles, clear procedures, proper handling and disposal of chemicals and living specimens, and knowing where safety equipment is. Students choose the right tool, such as a balance, graduated cylinder or thermometer, measure in metric units, understand precision and error, and present data in tables and graphs.

Key terms: lab safety (实验安全), goggles (护目镜), metric system (公制), precision (精度), measurement error (测量误差), graduated cylinder (量筒).

COMPETENCY 002 — HISTORY AND NATURE OF SCIENCE

要点：设计探究式调查，提出问题和假设，用证据推理，找误差来源，交流结果；科学伦理、同行评议与科学史。

Science is a way of knowing built on evidence. Students ask testable questions, form hypotheses, plan fair tests with one variable changed at a time, collect data, look for sources of error and communicate results. Scientific ideas change when new evidence appears, which is why peer review, honesty and the history of science matter.

Key terms: inquiry (探究), hypothesis (假设), variables (变量), fair test (公平测试), evidence (证据), peer review (同行评议).

COMPETENCY 003 — IMPACT OF SCIENCE

要点：伦理、经济和个人需要如何影响科学决策；健康与药物风险，人口增长，资源利用与枯竭，科学与全球挑战。

Science and technology change lives, and people must decide how to use them. Students weigh benefits and costs, consider health risks and substance use, and examine population growth, renewable and nonrenewable resources, pollution and conservation. Science helps societies face global challenges, but choices also involve ethics and economics.

Key terms: renewable resources (可再生资源), nonrenewable resources (不可再生资源), conservation (保护), risk and benefit (风险与收益), pollution (污染), ethics (伦理).

COMPETENCY 004 — CONCEPTS AND PROCESSES

要点：贯穿各学科的大概念：系统、证据与模型、变化与恒定、形态与功能；用规律解释和预测，评估模型的长处与局限。

A few big ideas run through all of science: systems and their parts, evidence and models, change and constancy, and form and function. Students use patterns to explain and predict, describe how parts of a system interact, and judge what a model shows well and what it leaves out.

Key terms: systems (系统), models (模型), patterns (规律), form and function (形态与功能), change and constancy (变化与恒定), prediction (预测).

COMPETENCY 005 — STUDENTS AS LEARNERS AND SCIENCE INSTRUCTION

要点：发展阶段、已有知识和态度如何影响科学学习；为所有学生调整教学，纠正迷思概念，动手设计与提问。

Children bring their own ideas about the world, and many are misconceptions, such as heavier objects falling faster. Teachers find out what students think, plan hands-on investigations that challenge those ideas, adapt instruction for English learners and students with special needs, ask open questions, and sequence lessons from the familiar to the abstract, often following a 5E cycle.

Key terms: misconceptions (迷思概念), prior knowledge (已有知识), 5E model (5E 教学模式), hands-on learning (动手学习), differentiation (差异化教学), questioning (提问).

COMPETENCY 006 — SCIENCE ASSESSMENT

要点：课程、教学与评估对齐；正式、非正式与表现性评估，评价项目与实验日志，向学生说明标准与结果。

Assessment in science checks both knowledge and the ability to investigate. Teachers align objectives, activities and assessments, and use quizzes, observations, performance tasks, projects, lab journals and rubrics. Sharing criteria in advance helps students know what quality looks like, and results shape the next lesson.

Key terms: alignment (对齐), performance assessment (表现性评估), rubric (评分量规), lab journal (实验日志), observation (观察), feedback (反馈).

COMPETENCY 007 — FORCES AND MOTION

要点：重力、电力、磁力；运动的测量与图像；不平衡力改变运动；简单机械与地质过程中的力。

Forces are pushes and pulls. Gravity, electric and magnetic forces act at a distance; friction acts between surfaces. Motion is described by position, speed and direction and can be graphed. Balanced forces leave motion unchanged; unbalanced forces change speed or direction, as Newton’s laws describe. Simple machines such as levers, pulleys and inclined planes trade force for distance.

Key terms: gravity (重力), friction (摩擦力), magnetism (磁力), speed (速度), Newton’s laws (牛顿定律), simple machines (简单机械), balanced forces (平衡力).

COMPETENCY 008 — PHYSICAL AND CHEMICAL PROPERTIES

要点：测量物质的物理性质；固液气三态；物理变化与化学变化；原子结构，元素、化合物、混合物与溶液。

Matter has properties we can observe and measure: mass, volume, density, color, magnetism and the ability to conduct heat. Solids, liquids and gases differ in how their particles move. A physical change, like melting, keeps the substance the same; a chemical change, like rusting, makes a new one. Atoms build elements and compounds; mixtures and solutions can be separated by physical means.

Key terms: states of matter (物质三态), density (密度), physical change (物理变化), chemical change (化学变化), atom (原子), mixtures (混合物), solutions (溶液).

COMPETENCY 009 — ENERGY AND INTERACTIONS

要点：能量守恒与转化；热传递与相变；电与磁；光和声的行为；它们在日常装置中的作用。

Energy cannot be created or destroyed, only changed from one form to another. Heat moves by conduction, convection and radiation and drives changes of state. Electric circuits need a closed path; series and parallel circuits behave differently. Light reflects, refracts and is absorbed; sound is a vibration that travels through a medium.

Key terms: conservation of energy (能量守恒), conduction (传导), convection (对流), radiation (辐射), circuits (电路), reflection (反射), refraction (折射).

COMPETENCY 010 — ENERGY TRANSFORMATIONS AND CONSERVATION

要点：电能的来源与转化；热、光、声、光合作用、天气和食物网中的能量传递；生命与地球科学中的物质与能量守恒。

Energy flows through every system. Power plants turn chemical, nuclear, wind or solar energy into electricity. Plants capture light energy in photosynthesis, and that energy passes through food chains while matter cycles. The sun’s energy drives weather. Across life, earth and space science, matter and energy are conserved.

Key terms: energy transformation (能量转化), photosynthesis (光合作用), food web (食物网), electricity (电能), solar energy (太阳能), conservation of matter (物质守恒).

COMPETENCY 011 — STRUCTURE AND FUNCTION OF LIVING THINGS

要点：从细胞到个体的组织层次，动植物生命周期（含变态发育），生物的需要，人体系统与分类。

Living things are organized from cells to tissues, organs, organ systems and organisms. Plants and animals go through life cycles; insects such as butterflies show complete metamorphosis. All organisms need energy, water, air and space. Human body systems work together, and scientists classify organisms by shared characteristics.

Key terms: cells (细胞), organ systems (器官系统), life cycle (生命周期), metamorphosis (变态发育), classification (分类), basic needs (基本需要).

COMPETENCY 012 — REPRODUCTION AND THE MECHANISMS OF HEREDITY

要点：动植物如何繁殖；遗传信息如何代代相传；遗传性状与习得行为；基因、显性与隐性；遗传与环境的共同作用。

Organisms reproduce sexually or asexually and pass genetic information to their offspring. Inherited traits, such as eye color, come from genes; learned behaviors, such as reading, do not. Dominant and recessive alleles explain why traits can skip a generation, and Punnett squares predict outcomes. Many traits depend on both genes and environment.

Key terms: inherited traits (遗传性状), learned behaviors (习得行为), genes (基因), dominant (显性), recessive (隐性), Punnett square (庞氏方格).

COMPETENCY 013 — ADAPTATIONS AND EVOLUTION

要点：适应如何帮助生存；种群与物种如何随时间改变；选择育种和突变如何改变性状。

Adaptations are structures or behaviors that help an organism survive in its environment, such as camouflage, thick fur or migration. Over many generations, individuals with helpful traits survive and reproduce more, so populations change: this is natural selection. Humans change traits on purpose through selective breeding, and mutations add new variation.

Key terms: adaptation (适应), camouflage (伪装), natural selection (自然选择), selective breeding (选择育种), mutation (突变), variation (变异).

COMPETENCY 014 — ORGANISMS AND THE ENVIRONMENT

要点：对刺激的反应，对环境的依赖，竞争、食物链与食物网，种群控制、生态位，以及生物如何改造生态系统。

Organisms respond to stimuli and depend on their surroundings and on each other. Producers, consumers and decomposers form food chains and food webs. Competition, predation and limited resources control population size. Each species fills a niche, and organisms, including people, change ecosystems, for example when beavers build dams.

Key terms: stimulus and response (刺激与反应), producers (生产者), consumers (消费者), decomposers (分解者), food chain (食物链), ecosystem (生态系统), niche (生态位).

COMPETENCY 015 — STRUCTURE AND FUNCTION OF EARTH SYSTEMS

要点：地球的圈层结构、板块构造、风化与侵蚀、地表水与地下水、大气组成，以及自然事件和人类活动对地球的改变。

Earth has a crust, mantle and core, and its surface is broken into moving plates that build mountains and cause earthquakes and volcanoes. Weathering breaks rock down; erosion and deposition move and lay it down, shaping landforms such as canyons and deltas. Surface water and groundwater, the atmosphere’s layers and gases, and human activity all shape Earth systems.

Key terms: crust, mantle and core (地壳、地幔、地核), plate tectonics (板块构造), weathering (风化), erosion (侵蚀), deposition (沉积), groundwater (地下水), atmosphere (大气).

COMPETENCY 016 — CYCLES IN EARTH SYSTEMS

要点：岩石循环与水循环；岩石、矿物与土壤的形成与性质；碳和氮等营养循环；人类影响与地球材料的利用。

Earth recycles its materials. In the rock cycle, igneous, sedimentary and metamorphic rocks change into one another through melting, weathering, pressure and heat. In the water cycle, water evaporates, condenses, precipitates and collects. Carbon and nitrogen cycle through living things, air and soil, and human use of Earth materials affects these cycles.

Key terms: rock cycle (岩石循环), igneous (火成岩), sedimentary (沉积岩), metamorphic (变质岩), water cycle (水循环), evaporation (蒸发), condensation (凝结).

COMPETENCY 017 — ENERGY IN WEATHER AND CLIMATE

要点：天气要素及其测量，天气与气候的区别，读天气图，驱动天气的能量传递，地球位置与地表特征如何塑造气候。

Weather is the condition of the atmosphere now; climate is the pattern over many years. Temperature, air pressure, humidity, wind and precipitation are measured with thermometers, barometers, hygrometers, anemometers and rain gauges. Uneven heating by the sun drives winds, fronts and the water cycle, and latitude, oceans and mountains shape a region’s climate.

Key terms: weather (天气), climate (气候), air pressure (气压), humidity (湿度), fronts (锋面), barometer (气压计), latitude (纬度).

COMPETENCY 018 — SOLAR SYSTEM AND THE UNIVERSE

要点：天空中的天体；地月日系统：昼夜、四季、月相、日食月食；太阳系各组成部分的性质。

Earth’s rotation on its axis causes day and night; its tilt as it revolves around the sun causes the seasons. The moon’s phases come from how much of its lit half we see, and eclipses happen when the sun, Earth and moon line up. The solar system has the sun, eight planets, moons, asteroids and comets, and the sun is one star among billions.

Key terms: rotation (自转), revolution (公转), axial tilt (地轴倾斜), seasons (四季), moon phases (月相), eclipse (日食月食), planets (行星).

SUBJECT EXAM V — FINE ARTS, HEALTH AND PHYSICAL EDUCATION (905)

艺术、健康与体育：40 题，35 分，约占总分 19%。共 5 条能力项：001 视觉艺术；002 音乐；003 健康；004 体育；005 戏剧。

COMPETENCY 001 — VISUAL ARTS

要点：艺术的元素与原则，各种媒材与技法，知觉的发展，评析作品，多元文化中的艺术，以及与其他学科的整合。

The elements of art are line, shape, form, color, value, texture and space; the principles of design, such as balance, contrast, emphasis, pattern, rhythm and unity, organize them. Children explore drawing, painting, printmaking, sculpture and collage, talk about artworks by describing, analyzing, interpreting and judging them, and meet art from many cultures.

Key terms: elements of art (艺术元素), principles of design (设计原则), color wheel (色环), media (媒材), art criticism (艺术评析), cultural heritage (文化遗产).

COMPETENCY 002 — MUSIC

要点：音乐读写与记谱，音乐术语，演唱演奏与简单创作，评价音乐，各种风格与文化中的音乐，及其与其他学科的联系。

Music literacy means reading and writing rhythm and pitch: notes and rests, the staff, meter and tempo. Students sing, play classroom instruments, move and create simple patterns and melodies. They describe music with terms such as dynamics, timbre and form, compare music of different times and cultures, and connect music to language, mathematics and history.

Key terms: rhythm (节奏), pitch (音高), tempo (速度), dynamics (力度), timbre (音色), form (曲式), staff notation (五线谱).

COMPETENCY 003 — HEALTH

要点：人体系统与疾病，营养、体能与成长，压力管理，药物与暴力预防，安全与急救，决策、媒体影响与健康职业。

Health education helps children make choices that keep them well. Topics include body systems, preventing disease, nutrition and food labels, growth and development, managing stress and emotions, refusing tobacco, alcohol and drugs, preventing bullying and violence, and basic safety and first aid. Students practise decision-making and learn to question media messages.

Key terms: nutrition (营养), disease prevention (疾病预防), stress management (压力管理), refusal skills (拒绝技巧), first aid (急救), decision-making (决策).

COMPETENCY 004 — PHYSICAL EDUCATION

要点：体能原理与积极生活，体能评估，运动技能发展，规则与公平竞赛，战术，为不同需要的学生调整活动。

Physical education builds movement skills and lifelong habits. Children develop locomotor skills like running and skipping, nonlocomotor skills like bending and twisting, and manipulative skills like throwing, catching and kicking. Fitness has five components and follows the FITT principle. Lessons teach rules, fair play, simple tactics and safety, and adapt activities so every child can take part.

Key terms: locomotor skills (位移动作), manipulative skills (操控动作), health-related fitness (健康体能), FITT principle (FITT 原则), sportsmanship (体育精神), adapted activities (调整活动).

COMPETENCY 005 — THEATRE

要点：戏剧的元素与惯例，诠释与表演，道具、布景、音响、服装、灯光等技术元素，戏剧职业，与历史文化及其他学科的联系。

Theatre teaches children to imagine, cooperate and communicate. They explore character, setting, conflict and plot through dramatic play, pantomime, improvisation and readers theatre. Technical theatre adds props, scenery, costumes, sound and lighting. Students learn audience etiquette, theatre jobs, the history of theatre in different cultures, and how drama can bring other subjects to life.

Key terms: dramatic play (戏剧游戏), pantomime (哑剧), improvisation (即兴), character (角色), plot (情节), technical theatre (舞台技术), audience etiquette (观众礼仪).
