Lake Bled · Autumn Sunset (scenic/assets/bled/)
  Photograph: "Bled Island 06.jpg" by Krzysztof Golik, 13 October 2021
  Source: https://commons.wikimedia.org/wiki/File:Bled_Island_06.jpg
  License: CC BY-SA 4.0 — https://creativecommons.org/licenses/by-sa/4.0/
  Changes by Minest: resized to 4096 / 2048 px, colour-graded toward an autumn sunset (foliage hue toward gold,
  warm light, sunset gradient over the sky, alpenglow on snow, warm sheen on the lake). Nothing painted in or removed.
  Layers: fg_2k.png is the near branch cut out with a soft alpha; bg_4k.webp / bg_2k.webp are the photo with that
  branch's pixels filled in from around it (a pyramid fill — the only place pixels are made, and only where the branch
  was). bled_2k.webp is the graded photo whole (cover / first frame).
  depth_bg_1024.png / depth_fg_1024.png: relative depth estimated offline from the graded photo with Depth Anything V2
  Small (onnx-community/depth-anything-v2-small, Apache-2.0), then cleaned (median, blur; the branch smoothed to one
  sheet). fx_1024.png: sky / lake masks made by Minest from the photo.
  All of these derived files are shared under the same license, CC BY-SA 4.0.
Ambient sound: synthesised in the browser (no recordings).
