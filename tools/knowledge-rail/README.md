# Knowledge Train & Knowledge Metro: standalone prototypes (2026-10-30)

These are not in the Minest pages, and the Knowledge Drive is unchanged. Preview them on the minest-44 server at `/rail/train.html` and `/rail/metro.html` (files copied into mn31/rail). Add `?fresh` to ignore saved progress.

- **`rail-common.js`** is GENERATED by `build_common.py` from `../knowledge-drive/drive.js`. It is Drive's data layer (provider, card info), billboard art, the board's back, the low-poly kit and the 4 landscapes. Re-run the script after drive.js changes.
- **`train.js`**: seated by a carriage window. The carriage (wall with a rounded window, wood frame, table, cup and book, curtains, seat, luggage rack) has its own camera.
  - **Outside:** each List is a railway line, and each line starts at a station (platform, canopy in the line colour, name sign). Cards stand outside as billboards or as houses with a mural, and a stack start is a blue sign. Scenery has parallax and uses the 4 landscapes.
  - **Window Focus:** tap a card and the train eases to a stop with it framed in the window. Tap it again to flip it to the meaning. ▶, or a tap on the scenery, resumes the journey.
  - **Controls:**
    - Auto Journey (Space)
    - Next Station (N)
    - Journey Map (M): every line as a track of stops, with progress; tap a line to travel there
    - Scenic Suggest (L) and ↺
    - Speed
  - **Saved progress:** `minest.train.v1`.
- **`metro.js`**: standing on a low-poly platform, seen at an angle. It has a track pit with rails and sleepers, a tiled wall with a stripe in the line colour, ceiling light strips and columns.
  - **The wall:** the station name board (line number, station = card title, ← previous, next →, ⇄ transfer), the card's poster, and a poster of the next station. A hanging LED screen shows the line, the next station and the train status.
  - **Trains:** three cars with the line-colour stripe and sliding doors. The flow is wait → arriving (ease-in) → doors open → Board (tap the train or the button) → tunnel fade → the next station, where the train departs.
  - **Smart:** trains come and go on their own; ⏸ pauses that.
  - **Transfers** are deterministic: a line's middle station meets the next line's first station.
  - **Other controls:**
    - Choose Line (L)
    - Metro Map (M): a staircase schematic with vertical interchanges; tap any station
    - Transfer (T)
    - Suggest station style (S): Classic Tile, Modern, Art Deco, Night Line
  - **Posters:** tap one to look at it, tap again to flip it.
  - **Saved progress:** `minest.metro.v1` (line, station, style).
- Both read cards live through the provider and never copy or write Kanban. Both pause while the page is hidden.
## In My World (2026-10-30)
- The My World 🚗 button opens **Presentation Gallery → Dynamic**, which has three mode cards: Drive, Train and Metro. The chosen mode is remembered in `minest.dynamic.mode`.
- Each mode offers the same starting points (this List, this Board, Workspace), with counts in that mode's words and its own ↺ Resume line.
- The style choices follow the mode: landscapes for Drive and Train, station styles for Metro.
- **Metro limits:** at most 8 lines. Interchanges link neighbouring lines at the same position. The Metro Map has one row per line and scrolls sideways on big boards.
- Line names drop trailing counts, so "Mathematics (28)" becomes "Mathematics".
- `knowledge-drive/patch_knowledge_drive.py` rebuilds rail-common.js and injects drive + rail-common + train + metro + entry (with their CSS) into all 9 entries.
- Not committed and not deployed.

# Knowledge Skyscraper: standalone prototype (2026-10-30)
This is not in the Minest pages. Preview it at `/rail/tower.html` (with `tower-sample.js`, a Science board of 6 lists with 6–9 cards each); add `?fresh` to start clean.

**`tower.js` (MinestTower)**

The Board is a glass tower in a night skyline: instanced city blocks with lit windows, stars, and a plaza with trees.

**Smart Floor Generation**
- 1F is the Knowledge Lobby, with a reception desk, a Directory you can tap to ride, and the tower's name over the elevator.
- Each List gets a floor. A List with more than 10 cards spans several floors ("Ecology · 1/2"), small Lists share a floor ("Movies & Albums"), and an empty List becomes an empty hall.
- The roof is the Discovery Observatory: a glass dome, a telescope, and stands holding picture cards from across the board.

**Exhibitions per floor:** Word Wall, Bookshelf, Museum Gallery, Cabinet of Knowledge, Sculpture Gallery, Memory Orb Archive.
- The first choice comes from the cards (book → Bookshelf, person/poster/album → Gallery, text → Word Wall), then variety, with no two floors the same at first.
- Fixtures are simple low-poly pieces built here; the prototype has no My World asset catalogue.

**Navigation**
- **Elevator:** a brushed-metal panel with round buttons, a display and ▲▼. Inside a floor, a ride closes steel doors, rolls the floor numbers (about 1.4 s however far you go) and opens on the new floor. Tap the doors to skip. Keyboard: ↑ ↓ PgUp PgDn, 1–9, R.
- **Cutaway (C):** the front and right glass fade and their mullions go, so the floors show like a dollhouse. Tap a floor to fly straight in. The elevator car rides the core.
- **Skyline (K):** the camera pulls back to the outside and slowly orbits. Drag to orbit, wheel or pinch to zoom.

**Items:** tap one and it slides out to you; tap again to flip it to the meaning, notes and checklist; tap anywhere to put it back.

**Suggest**
- In Skyline it changes the exterior: Azure Glass, Champagne, Pearl White or Obsidian.
- In Cutaway it restyles every unlocked floor (never matching the floor above or below) and the exterior.
- In a floor it restyles that floor. 🔒 Lock keeps a floor's exhibition.

**Performance:** only the current floor and its neighbours get card art; other floors show plain colour, and inside, far floors' exhibitions are hidden. The texture cache is capped.

**Saved separately (never in Kanban):** `minest.tower.v1`, holding each floor's exhibition style and lock, the exterior and the last floor.

# Dynamic Presentation Phase 2 (Rack / Library / Museum): prototypes done 2026-10-31
Standalone prototypes; not wired into My World, not committed, not deployed. Pages: `rack.html`, `library.html`, `museum.html` (add `?fresh` to ignore saved state).
- `showcase-common.js` (KShow) is the shared layer for all three:
  - data: `collect()` turns a Board / List / Stack into Card refs grouped by List, read live through rail-common.js.
  - stage: renderer, loop that pauses when hidden, taps vs drags, toast, `.reading` state.
  - reader: Pull Out → fly to you → tap to Flip → tap outside → Return to its place.
  - carriers: book, index card, memory orb, badge, frame, label plate.
  - store: per-source saved state.
- `rack.js` (MinestRack): Rotate → Select → Pull → Read → Resume.
  - 12 slots; book / orb / badge carriers.
  - Suggest cycles ring → tower → two tiers; Material button cycles walnut / metal / glass.
  - Saved in `minest.rack.v1`.
- `library.js` (MinestLibrary): Walk → Browse → Pull → Read → Return.
  - One section per List, with a hanging brass sign and a ladder; Card books carry spine titles among instanced filler books.
  - Category Jump; Suggest cycles Walnut Hall / Oak Archive / Night Reading.
  - Real books are built only within a few bays of you. 1200 cards: 59 ms build, 60 fps.
  - Saved in `minest.library.v1`.
- `museum.js` (MinestMuseum): Walk → Discover → Focus → Read → Continue.
  - Halls are 🖼 Gallery / 🗿 Sculpture / 🔎 Cabinet. Smart picks a theme from the pictures in a hall; Suggest re-themes.
  - Guided Tour (4 s at each exhibit), Free Walk (drag, or tap the floor), tap → focus → tap → pull, Resume Tour, Tour Map (SVG; vertical on phones).
  - Capped at 12 halls × 12 exhibits; exhibits are built in the current hall and its neighbours.
  - Saved in `minest.museum.v1`. The sample page shows 3 halls × 5 exhibits.
- To wire into My World later: add MODES entries in knowledge-drive/entry.js and inject showcase-common.js + the three files in patch_knowledge_drive.py (same pattern as tower).
